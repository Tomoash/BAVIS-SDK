# Getting help / Kde hledat pomoc

* **Questions about writing a driver** – [Discussions → Q&A](https://github.com/Tomoash/BAVIS-SDK/discussions/categories/q-a).
  Please include the BAVIS version (`bavis-sdk info`), what you tried and the output of `bavis-sdk check`.
* **A bug in the SDK, a guide or an example** – an issue with the template *Bug report*.
* **A device or protocol you need** – [Discussions → Drivers](https://github.com/Tomoash/BAVIS-SDK/discussions/categories/drivers).
* **Your BAVIS installation, licences, commercial support** – [benesl@benesl-automation.cz](mailto:benesl@benesl-automation.cz),
  [benesl-automation.cz](https://benesl-automation.cz).
* **Security vulnerabilities** – privately, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Česky: dotazy k psaní driverů patří do [Discussions → Q&A](https://github.com/Tomoash/BAVIS-SDK/discussions/categories/q-a)
(uveďte verzi BAVIS z `bavis-sdk info`, co jste zkusili a výstup `bavis-sdk check`), chyby do issue se šablonou *Bug report*,
žádosti o zařízení do [Discussions → Drivers](https://github.com/Tomoash/BAVIS-SDK/discussions/categories/drivers). Instalace,
licence a placená podpora: [benesl@benesl-automation.cz](mailto:benesl@benesl-automation.cz).
